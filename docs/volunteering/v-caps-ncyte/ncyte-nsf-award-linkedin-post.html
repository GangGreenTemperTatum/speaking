🚀 BIG NEWS! NCyTE at Whatcom Community College (WCC) Awarded $1.07 Million to Expand AI and Cybersecurity Career Opportunities for High School Students! The future of cybersecurity starts with the next generation! 🔐🤖 The National Cybersecurity Training and Education Resource Center (NCyTE) at WCC has been awarded a $1.07 million supplemental grant from the National Science Foundation to expand artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity career opportunities for high school students nationwide. Through the Virtual Cybersecurity Advanced Placement Course Supplemental (V-CAPS) program, developed in partnership with Bugcrowd students will participate in a 12-week virtual, hands-on learning experience that goes beyond the classroom. Students will have the opportunity to: ✅ Gain 80+ hours of hands-on cybersecurity experience ✅ Work in teams to tackle real-world cybersecurity challenges ✅ Learn from top-tier active security researchers ✅ Build ethical hacking and vulnerability research skills ✅ Receive a Certificate of Completion demonstrating applied work-based learning ✅ Eligible for a stipend upon successful completion of program milestones This innovative initiative connects education with industry, giving students meaningful opportunities to explore high-demand technical careers while helping build a stronger, more diverse cybersecurity and AI workforce. We're excited to help shape the next generation of cybersecurity professionals and open doors to emerging technology careers! Learn more about V-CAPS and student participation opportunities: https://ow.ly/UNMW5105vl9 #NCyTE #WhatcomCommunityCollege #NSF #CybersecurityEducation #ArtificialIntelligence #AIWorkforce #CybersecurityCareers #WorkforceDevelopment #VulnerabilityResearch #FutureOfCybersecurity #Bugcrowd #NCyTE #WCC
