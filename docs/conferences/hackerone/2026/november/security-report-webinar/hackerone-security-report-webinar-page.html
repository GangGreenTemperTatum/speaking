The Vulnerability Is Validated. The Clock Is Running. Now What?

November 4, 2026, at 12 PM ET

Register Now

Your team is remediating vulnerabilities 54% faster than two years ago. Your open backlog has still grown 131% in the same period.

This session is for the security leaders who need to close the gap. 

That paradox is the defining security challenge of 2026, and it has a name: Exposure Debt.

Join industry experts for a data-driven look at what the 10th annual HackerOne Security Research Report reveals about how AI is reshaping vulnerability discovery, who bears accountability when it goes wrong, and why organizations that are finding more and fixing faster are still falling further behind.

In this session:

  • Why nearly 7 in 10 security leaders say validated findings are entering their backlog faster than their teams can fix them
  • How 85% of researchers upskilling in AI is changing what gets found, and how serious it is
  • What happens when AI gets it wrong: 39% of CISOs take the blame, and fewer than 10% have a framework for it
  • Five actions the data supports to start closing the gap
