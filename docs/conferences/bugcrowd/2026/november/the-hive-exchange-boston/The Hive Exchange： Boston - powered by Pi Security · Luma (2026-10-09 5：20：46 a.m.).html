About Event

​The Hive Exchange is coming to Boston this November!

​Boston has always been a hub for cybersecurity innovation, starting when the legendary hacking group, L0pht, pushed the boundaries of hacking in the 1990s at MIT. To celebrate this legacy, we’re bringing together some of the brightest minds of security in the heart of Boston for a night of networking, insights about the future of hacking and AI defense, and fun.

​Join Andrew Wilson (Bugcrowd), Vishal Chauhan (Pi Security), and AI Hacker Ads Dawson for an intimate conversation about the future of offensive and defensive security. This session explores the real-world shift happening across the industry:

  • ​How tradecraft has evolved over the last 18 months as hackers build AI sidekicks into their workflows.

  • ​Why human context still rules when evaluating vulnerability severity and business risk.

  • ​What companies must change tomorrow to earn trust with elite researchers while scaling their security operations alongside AI.
