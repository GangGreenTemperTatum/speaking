02 / ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

For the people

NahamCon is where the hacker community comes to hear what’s next.

Founded by NahamSec, the conference spotlights offensive security, bug bounties, and the people pushing the field forward. This edition comes to a live stage in Oakland while the talks are streamed for the global community. It is the conference portion of Prompt2Pwn, a larger event built around live AI hacking.
